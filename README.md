## About

I'm interested in what makes people tick, what moves culture and why certain ideas endure. My work sits at the intersection of **consumer behaviour, cultural research, audience intelligence and marketing strategy**.

I explore the cultural undercurrents shaping mainstream behaviour — from emerging subcultures and music scenes to digital communities, identity and brand relationships. I combine qualitative curiosity with data-led thinking to uncover patterns, understand audiences and inform creative and marketing decisions.

My experience spans **Google, COPA90, Barclays, Bullion Productions, Def Jam and Sony Music Records**, with projects connected to **Arsenal FC, BBC Sport, ITV, Nike and UEFA**, among others.

## Selected impact
